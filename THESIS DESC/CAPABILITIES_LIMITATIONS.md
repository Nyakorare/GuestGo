 # Capabilities and Limitations of the GuestGo Web-Based System
 
 ## Capabilities
 The following are the capabilities of the developed GuestGo web-based visitor management, gate access, and AI face verification system, with emphasis on automation, integration, and AI-driven workflows:
 1. Automates end-to-end visitor scheduling and registration by collecting guest details, enforcing place-specific rules (advance notice, limits), handling email verification, and generating visit QR codes without manual encoding by staff.
 2. Connects the Home, Dashboard, and Track Schedule pages into a unified flow so that a visit created on the Home page is automatically visible in dashboards (per role) and in the Track Schedule page via visit ID and QR code.
 3. Automates gate processing by linking QR scans and AI-based face capture to Supabase RPCs that validate visit status, update lifecycle states (pending, in_progress, temporary_exit, completed, completed_flagged, unsuccessful), and log all actions for audit purposes.
 4. Uses AI-based face detection and verification as a core security layer: the Python microservice (YOLOv8 + MediaPipe) and the TensorFlow.js BlazeFace fallback automatically detect faces from live camera frames, crop them, compress and encrypt them, and compute similarity scores between entrance and exit images to support automatic verification decisions.
 5. Integrates facial AI tightly with scheduling and gate operations so that faces optionally enrolled during scheduling are later used for comparison and review in guard dashboards, visit tracking, and administrative face data modals.
 6. Automates visit limit enforcement by computing weekly visit counts per user (Philippine timezone), checking place-level visit limits, and blocking or allowing new schedules accordingly, while updating UI elements such as weekly visit status banners and disabling/enabling scheduling controls.
 7. Provides role-based dashboards (admin, log, personnel, guard, visitor) that automatically surface the data and tools each role needs—such as active visits, logs, reschedule requests, gate scans, and feedback—while hiding irrelevant features, ensuring that data flows are tailored but still come from a single, shared backend.
 8. Automates notifications and communication using integrated email services: visit confirmations and completions with embedded QR codes are generated and sent via Brevo, while OTP codes and contact form messages flow through EmailJS, all driven by system events rather than manual emails.
 9. Links feedback collection to the visit lifecycle by triggering ISO 25010 surveys after visit completion, enforcing one-response-per-visit, storing scores in the database, and surfacing analytics in the dashboard and testimonials on the Contact page without manual aggregation.
 10. Unifies QR services so that the same QR encoding/decoding utilities are reused across scheduling (generation), guard operations (scanning and validation), QR Scanner page (read-only viewing), and Track Schedule (QR display and printing), ensuring consistent data and behavior across modules.
 11. Automates audit logging for all critical operations (visit creation, updates, gate scans, AI decisions, limits enforcement, reschedules), and centralizes logs in a dedicated dashboard for log-role users, connecting low-level actions from multiple modules into a single observable timeline.
 12. Coordinates place and personnel management with scheduling and gate operations so that changes to places, purposes, limits, and personnel assignments are immediately reflected in what visitors can schedule and what personnel and guards see at gates and in dashboards.
 13. Provides automatic handling of edge cases such as auto-creating “visitor” roles during gate entrance when missing, normalizing past-due visits via server-side status-fix routines, and displaying pending feedback/reschedule notifications when users log in to their dashboards.
 14. Integrates performance and monitoring hooks—such as adaptive scan intervals, FPS/latency indicators, and AI status dashboards—to automatically tune scanning frequency and expose service health without requiring users to manage low-level performance settings.
 15. Ties together all user-facing pages (Home, About, Contact, Dashboard per role, QR Scanner, Track Schedule) so that small features like FAQs, workflow steppers, public feedback rotation, printable cards, and PH clock collectively support the main automated workflows rather than operating in isolation.
 
 ## Limitations
 The following are the generalized limitations of the developed GuestGo web-based system:
 1. The system is dependent on network connectivity and multiple external cloud services (Supabase, Brevo, EmailJS, Python AI service); degraded connectivity or misconfiguration of environment keys can limit or disable core automated workflows (auth, scheduling, emails, and AI).
 2. AI-powered face detection and verification improve security but are constrained by camera quality, lighting conditions, and the absence of liveness/spoof detection, so final security still requires human oversight and policy enforcement at gates.
 3. Data handling and encryption for facial images prioritize practicality and storage efficiency (compression + XOR obfuscation) rather than strong, standards-based cryptography, so additional hardening would be required for stricter biometric compliance regimes.
 4. Scheduling logic is intentionally restrictive—using Philippine timezone, fixed weekly visit limits, place-level advance notice, and a 1‑month booking window—so the system is optimized for a specific institutional policy model and is less flexible for other organizations or timezones without further configuration.
 5. Certain operational conveniences are not yet fully automated, such as export/report generation and richer availability modeling (multi-day or recurring unavailability), which means some administrative analysis and planning still rely on manual review of dashboards.
 6. Guest and visitor experiences rely on QR codes, camera access, and visit IDs delivered via email; users who lack camera access, lose their visit IDs, or cannot use Gmail for OTP may face reduced convenience or require staff assistance.
 7. While the system supports multiple roles with tailored dashboards, some advanced policy variations—like dynamic overrides of limits per role or organization-specific escalation flows—are not yet exposed through configuration and would require code or database changes to customize.

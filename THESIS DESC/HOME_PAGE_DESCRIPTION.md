# GuestGo Home Page Description

Figure X presents the landing page or home page of the web-based GuestGo visitor management system. The header navigation includes Home, About, Contact Us, Track Schedule, and role-based links such as Dashboard, Guard Dashboard, and QR Scanner, along with the GuestGo logo and authentication options. Below the navigation bar is the hero section featuring the main title "Welcome to GuestGo" with the tagline "Your one-stop solution for guest management and hospitality services," which introduces the system's purpose. When the user clicks the "Schedule Now" button, the system opens a scheduling modal that allows visitors to book appointments, select places to visit, specify visit purposes, and choose dates within the allowed timeframe. 

Following the hero section is the Weekly Visit Status display, which appears for logged-in visitor users and shows their current week's visit count, remaining visit slots (maximum 2 visits per week), active visits, and completed visits with week range information. This section helps users track their scheduling limits and plan their visits accordingly.

Below the hero section are three feature cards that highlight the system's key capabilities: Smart Scheduling, which emphasizes limit-aware, timezone-accurate, and fast booking; Secure Verification, which showcases email code checks and approvals; and Real-time Tracking, which displays status updates and QR scanning functionality. These cards provide users with a quick overview of the system's main features.

After the feature cards is the "How GuestGo Works" section, which presents an interactive workflow guide with role-specific steps. The workflow steps are displayed as clickable tabs that dynamically change based on the user's role (Visitor, Guest, Personnel, Guard, Admin, or Logs). Each role sees customized workflow panels that explain the specific processes they will follow, such as scheduling visits, scanning QR codes, managing gate operations, or overseeing system administration. This section helps users understand the step-by-step procedures relevant to their role in the system.

Following the workflow section is the Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) section, where users can learn about common queries regarding scheduling, verification methods, QR code functionality, visit limits, face detection, and other system features. The FAQ items are presented as expandable accordion panels with smooth animations, allowing users to click on questions to reveal detailed answers about the system's operations and capabilities.

At the bottom of the page, users can view the footer section which contains the company information, quick navigation links (Home, About, Contact Us, Support), and contact details including the physical address at University Campus, Computer Science Department, Building 2, Room 301, phone number (+63 912 345 6789), and email address (support@guestgo.com). The footer also includes social media links and legal information such as Privacy Policy, Documentations, and Terms of Service links, providing users with comprehensive access to support resources and company information.
